The sun

The sun rises roughly in the east and sets roughly in the west. In the Northern Hemisphere, at midday it is due south (in the Southern Hemisphere, due north).

Shadow stick

  1. Push a straight stick upright into flat ground. Mark the tip of its shadow with a stone.
  2. Wait 15–20 minutes and mark the new tip of the shadow.
  3. A line from the first mark to the second points roughly east; west is the opposite way. Stand with the first mark on your left and the second on your right: you are facing north.

North Star (Northern Hemisphere)

Find the Big Dipper. Follow the two stars at the end of its "cup" upward about five times the distance between them to reach Polaris, a moderately bright star alone in its area. Polaris is almost exactly due north.

Southern Cross (Southern Hemisphere)

Extend the long axis of the Southern Cross about four and a half times its length; the point below that spot on the horizon is roughly south.

Staying found

If lost, stop, stay calm, and stay put if people will look for you. Moving usually makes you harder to find. Downhill along streams often leads to roads and settlements, but can also lead into dangerous terrain.
