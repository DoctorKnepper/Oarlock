Carbon monoxide (CO) has no color or smell and can kill in minutes. Many deaths happen after storms, from generators and heaters used indoors.

Never run these indoors

Generators, gas or charcoal grills, camp stoves, and gasoline-powered tools must never be used inside a home, basement, garage, tent or crawl space — even with doors and windows open. Keep generators at least 6 m (20 feet) from windows, doors and vents.

Warning signs

Headache, dizziness, weakness, nausea, vomiting, chest pain and confusion — often several people or pets at once. Sleeping or drunk people can die before noticing.

If you suspect CO

Get everyone into fresh air immediately and call for help. Don't go back inside until the source is off and the area is aired out. Battery-powered CO alarms are the best protection.
