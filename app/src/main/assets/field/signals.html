Three of anything means distress

Three whistle blasts, three shots, three fires in a triangle, or three flashes of light, repeated after a pause, are recognized as a call for help.

SOS

··· ––– ··· (three short, three long, three short) by flashlight, mirror, whistle, horn or tapping. Send it as one continuous sequence, pause, then repeat.

Signal mirror

Hold the mirror near your eye, stretch out your other hand toward the aircraft or ship, and tilt the mirror so the reflected spot of sunlight lands on your fingers, then on the target. Sweep across the horizon even if you see no one — flashes can be seen many kilometres away.

Ground-to-air signals

Make large symbols (at least 3 m / 10 ft long) with contrasting material, rocks, branches or trampled snow:

SymbolMeaning
VRequire assistance
XRequire medical assistance
↑ (arrow)Proceeding in this direction

Radio

On marine VHF, channel 16 is the distress and calling channel. Say "Mayday, Mayday, Mayday", your position, the problem and how many people. On CB radio, channel 9 is reserved for emergencies in the US.
