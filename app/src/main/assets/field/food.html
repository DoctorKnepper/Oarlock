During a power outage

  • Keep fridge and freezer doors closed as much as possible.
  • A closed refrigerator keeps food safe for about 4 hours.
  • A full freezer holds its temperature for about 48 hours (about 24 hours if half full).
  • Use an appliance thermometer if you have one: food in a fridge is safe at 4°C (40°F) or below.

After the power returns

Throw out perishable food — meat, poultry, fish, eggs, milk, soft cheese and leftovers — that has been above 4°C (40°F) for more than 2 hours. Frozen food that still has ice crystals or is at 4°C (40°F) or below can be refrozen or cooked.

When in doubt, throw it out. Never taste food to decide whether it is safe.

Food that touched floodwater

Discard any food that isn't in a waterproof container, including cardboard boxes, screw-capped jars and home-canned food. Undamaged commercial cans can be washed, sanitized and kept.
