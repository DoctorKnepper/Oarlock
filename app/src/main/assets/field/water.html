Use bottled or stored water first. Treat any water from rivers, lakes, wells or floodwater before drinking, cooking, brushing teeth or washing dishes.

Boiling (best method)

Let cloudy water settle, then filter it through a clean cloth or coffee filter. Bring it to a rolling boil for 1 minute — or 3 minutes above 2,000 m (6,500 ft). Let it cool and store it covered.

Household bleach

Use plain, unscented chlorine bleach (no added cleaners or scents). Check the label for the strength, then add per gallon (about 3.8 litres) of clear water:

Bleach strengthPer 1 gallon
6% sodium hypochlorite8 drops (about 1/8 teaspoon)
8.25% sodium hypochlorite6 drops

Stir, wait at least 30 minutes. The water should have a slight chlorine smell; if not, repeat the dose and wait another 15 minutes. If the water is cloudy, filter it first and double the dose.

Other methods

  • Water purification tablets: follow the package directions exactly.
  • Portable filters remove bacteria and parasites but most don't remove viruses; combine with chemical treatment where viruses are a concern.
  • Bleach and boiling do not remove chemicals, fuel or heavy metals. Avoid water that smells of fuel or chemicals.

Storage

Store at least 1 gallon (4 litres) per person per day for drinking and sanitation, for several days. Use clean food-grade containers with tight lids.
