Priorities

  • Get out of wind and wet first. Wet clothing loses most of its warmth; stay dry.
  • Insulate yourself from the ground — it draws heat out of your body faster than the air does. Use branches, leaves, cardboard or a pack.
  • Keep shelters small: your body heat has to warm the space.

Staying warm at home without power

  • Close off unused rooms; gather everyone in one room, ideally with few windows.
  • Cover windows with blankets at night; open curtains on sunny windows by day.
  • Wear layers, a hat and dry socks. Eat and drink — your body needs fuel to make heat.
  • Use only heaters designed for indoor use, and never fuel-burning devices meant for outdoors (see Carbon Monoxide).

Outdoors

A quick debris shelter: lean a long branch against a stump or rock, lay sticks along both sides, then pile leaves and brush thickly on top — at least an arm's length deep — and fill the inside with dry leaves to lie on.
