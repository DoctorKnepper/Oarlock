If the person can cough forcefully or speak, encourage them to keep coughing. Act when they cannot breathe, cough or speak.

Adults and children over 1 year

  1. Stand behind them. Give up to 5 firm back blows between the shoulder blades with the heel of your hand (the method used by the Red Cross).
  2. Then give up to 5 abdominal thrusts: make a fist just above the navel, grab it with your other hand, and pull sharply inward and upward.
  3. Repeat until the object comes out or the person becomes unresponsive. (The American Heart Association teaches abdominal thrusts alone; either approach is acceptable.)
  4. For pregnant or large people, give chest thrusts on the center of the breastbone instead.

If the person becomes unresponsive, lower them to the ground, call for help and start CPR. Look in the mouth before rescue breaths and remove any object you can see.

Infants under 1 year

Support the baby face-down along your forearm, head lower than the body. Give 5 back blows, then turn face-up and give 5 chest thrusts with two fingers in the center of the chest. Never do blind finger sweeps.
