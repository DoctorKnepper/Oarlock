Home kit (at least 3 days, ideally 2 weeks)

  • Water: 1 gallon (4 L) per person per day
  • Non-perishable food and a manual can opener
  • Prescription medicines, glasses, and a first-aid kit with a tourniquet
  • Flashlights, headlamp and spare batteries; battery or hand-crank radio
  • Phone charger and a power bank (charge your phone and Ark's downloads ahead of time)
  • Whistle, dust masks, plastic sheeting and duct tape
  • Wrench or pliers to turn off utilities; fire extinguisher; battery CO and smoke alarms
  • Warm clothing, sturdy shoes, blankets or sleeping bags
  • Hygiene supplies, garbage bags and moist towelettes
  • Copies of important documents in a waterproof bag; some cash
  • Pet food and supplies; items for babies or older family members
  • Paper maps of your area and a written list of important phone numbers

Car kit

  • Water, snacks, blankets, flashlight, first-aid kit
  • Jumper cables, tire repair kit, tow strap, small shovel
  • Ice scraper and kitty litter or sand for traction in winter
  • Phone charging cable and a reflective warning triangle

Make a plan

Agree on a meeting place near home and one outside your neighbourhood, and an out-of-area contact everyone can call or text. Practice how to leave quickly.
